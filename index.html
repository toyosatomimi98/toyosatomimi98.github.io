Papers

  • Driving Like Yourself: A Benchmark for Closed-Loop Personalized End-to-End Autonomous Driving

    ECCV 2026

    Xiaoru Dong, Ruiqin Li, Xiao Han, Zhenxuan Wu, Jiamin Wang, Jian Chen, Qi Jiang, S. M. Yiu, Xinge Zhu, Yuexin Ma

    Person2Drive is a benchmark and platform for personalized end-to-end autonomous driving. It comes with a data collection system for building personalized driving datasets, metrics based on MMD and KL divergence for quantifying individual driving styles, and a personalization framework that fine-tunes only the trajectory prediction head — so the pretrained backbone stays intact and driving performance is preserved.

    arXiv:2602.18757 · Google Scholar
